Prepared 5 October 2026

This adds optional passkey sign-in for business accounts, staff and the separate
Alport company-admin account. Password sign-in stays available. Face ID, Touch ID,
a device PIN or another supported method is chosen by the person's device.

2. LET RENDER DEPLOY THE COMPLETE COMMIT

The requirements file includes webauthn==3.0.1, a compatible cryptography version,
and pywebpush for the existing staff notification feature. Do not leave the old
requirements.txt in place: its cryptography pin conflicts with this update.
Database tables are created automatically in your existing PostgreSQL database
at app startup. There is no separate SQL file or database-reset step.

No new Meta, banking or paid-service registration is needed. Keep your current
DATABASE_URL, SECRET_KEY, COMPANY_ADMIN_EMAIL and COMPANY_ADMIN_PASSWORD values.
SECRET_KEY must be your existing private random key, not CHANGE_THIS_IN_RENDER.
Do not rotate it just to install this update: that signs everyone out and
invalidates the new passkeys.

By default passkeys work on BOTH:
  https://alporthospitality.co.uk
  https://www.alporthospitality.co.uk
They do not work on the Render preview hostname with this configuration.

3. ENABLE IT ON YOUR OWN PHONE

BUSINESS ACCOUNT OR STAFF:
- Open https://alporthospitality.co.uk in Safari on your iPhone.
- Sign in using the usual password (staff use /staff/login).
- Open Face ID & passkeys from the workspace, staff portal or People page.
  You can also visit https://alporthospitality.co.uk/passkeys after signing in.
- Name the passkey, e.g. Alex's iPhone, and enter your CURRENT account password.
- Press Confirm password, then Save passkey on this device.
- Complete the device's own prompt yourself.
- Sign out, then choose Face ID / passkey on the sign-in page.

COMPANY ADMIN:
- Sign in at https://alporthospitality.co.uk/company-admin/login using the
  separate company-admin credentials.
- Open Face ID & passkeys in the top bar, or visit
  https://alporthospitality.co.uk/company-admin/passkeys.
- Follow the same setup steps using your COMPANY-ADMIN password.
- That passkey signs in only through the company-admin sign-in page.

Each person enables their own passkey. Adding a staff member does not silently
register a passkey on their behalf. No camera permission is needed; the device
handles its own identity check. Alport does not receive face scans, fingerprints,
device PINs or passkey private keys.

4. RECOVERY AND DEVICE CHANGES

Your password stays usable. If you lose a device, sign in with your password on
another device, open Face ID & passkeys, then remove the lost passkey using your
current password. A synced passkey can exist on several devices: removing it in
Alport disables all copies of that credential. You can separately delete its
saved entry in your device's password manager.

Removing a passkey stops future sign-ins, but does not terminate already-open
sessions. Changing the account password invalidates its existing passkeys; add
a new passkey afterwards. This also applies to changes to company-admin email
or password in Render. Existing password recovery/invitation procedures remain.
Do not save an account passkey in a shared venue device/password manager.

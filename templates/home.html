The Alport platform

One operating view for the whole venue.

Built around the way hospitality businesses actually run. Alport connects operational information so managers spend less time moving between spreadsheets and disconnected systems, and more time running the business.

Management Dashboard

Bring revenue, costs, labour, operational performance and key venue information together in one management view.

Stock & Purchasing

Track stock holdings, supplier products and pack prices. Till-driven stock deductions and purchase ordering are planned additions.

Menu & Recipe Costing

Cost food and drink recipes component by component, record portion sizes and selling prices, and monitor gross profit.

People, Rota & Payroll

Manage staff, hourly rates, holiday allowances, FOH and BOH rotas, labour deployment and payroll information.

Finance & EBITDA

Record sales, expenses, invoices, payments, budgets and operating costs to understand profitability beyond headline turnover.

EHO & Food Safety

Opening and closing checks, temperatures, corrective actions, sign-offs, compliance records, management verification and audit views.

Forecasting — planned

Planned: combine historical trading with bookings, weather and local events to suggest stock orders and support demand planning.

Bookings & Integrations

Alport is being designed to connect with hospitality booking, EPOS and supplier systems so operational data can work together.

Multi-site Management

Operate multiple venues within the same organisation while maintaining site-level data, users, controls and reporting.

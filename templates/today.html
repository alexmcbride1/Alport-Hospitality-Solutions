        Help with this page

        What does EBITDA mean here?

        The estimate is sales minus recorded stock usage, scheduled labour and overheads, before interest, tax, depreciation and amortisation. Check data coverage and your accountant’s adjustments before using it for decisions.

        Why do figures differ from my till or bank?

        Sales must be entered or reconciled into the sales ledger. An imported till ledger may still await reconciliation. Bank receipts, VAT, stock purchases and wages paid can fall in different periods from these management figures.

        Who can see my information?

        Staff see their permitted sites and personal actions. Full site managers see their site’s dashboard. The business Owner can compare sites. Team chat history and tasks are shared with everyone who has room access.

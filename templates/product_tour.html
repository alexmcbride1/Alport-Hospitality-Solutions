A short tour of the workspace for hospitality owners, managers and site teams.

These are screenshots of the actual interface with fictional venues, people and records. They are examples, not customer results.

01 / DAILY OPERATIONS

Open the right site. See what needs attention.

Bring overdue checks, today’s bookings, scheduled staff, low stock and team tasks into one page. Each action leads to the relevant workspace section.

  • Owners can compare their sites.
  • Staff see personal actions for their permitted sites.
  • Phone-friendly shortcuts lead to shifts, checks, evidence and chat.
Open mobile screenshot

02 / SITE TEAMS

Turn a conversation into a task.

Keep each site’s conversation separate. Mention a colleague, check unread messages, then assign a message as a task with a responsible person and deadline.

  • Task status and update history show who did what.
  • Everyone with room access can read its messages and tasks.
  • Mentions appear in Alport; email and push alerts are not included for chat.
Open full chat screenshot

03 / BUSINESS VISIBILITY

See the numbers with their context.

Compare recorded sales, stock costs, scheduled labour and estimated EBITDA against budgets and the previous month. Source dates and missing-data notices help explain what the figures do—and don’t—show.

  • Clear warnings when records or budgets are missing.
  • Setup essentials link directly to the next step.
  • Owners can review business-wide record changes.

Management estimates use the workspace’s gross-value basis and recorded inputs. They are not statutory accounts or cash-profit figures.

Before you get started

Will staff see every site’s figures?

The daily workspace restricts staff to their active site assignments. Financial views require full site manager access; the business Owner can compare all their sites. Chat access alone does not grant financial access.

How do I get help?

The signed-in website includes a user guide, contextual explanations and a report-a-problem form. You can also contact Alport through the support form.

Can I try the interface before joining?

This walkthrough shows the real interface using fictional records without creating an account. Contact Alport to discuss a guided demonstration for your venues.
